## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the serene, revitalizing atmosphere of an Indonesian highland ecotourism village. It pairs rural tranquility with modern travel utility, addressing local and domestic Indonesian travelers who explore scenic destinations via mobile devices on the go.

The visual direction combines **Organic Minimalism** with warm, tactile mobile hospitality:
- **Atmospheric & Earthy:** Invokes morning mist over terraced tea plantations, wet foliage, and filtered golden hour sunlight.
- **Approachable & Welcoming:** Generously rounded corners, comfortable line heights, and thumb-friendly interactive zones deliver an unhurried, reassuring experience.
- **Utilitarian Clarity:** High contrast typography and distinct badge semantics ensure effortless navigation under harsh tropical daylight or one-handed handheld usage.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from lush highland agriculture and tropical morning light:

- **Primary (`#1B4D3E` - Forest Green):** Deep canopy green used for dominant actions, primary button fills, navigation bars, and anchor titles. It conveys stability, conservation, and dense highland foliage.
- **Secondary (`#D99B26` - Golden Sunlight):** Warm harvest gold for promotional highlights, rating stars, booking action accents, active states, and alert tags.
- **Tertiary (`#2E7D5A` - Emerald Tea Leaf):** Vibrant mountain vegetation tone applied to informational indicators, verified badges, active filters, and success notifications.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FAF7F0` - Broken White / Warm Cream):** Soft, organic base replacing harsh pure white to reduce outdoor glare and provide a cozy, hospitable atmosphere.

On-surface text relies on a deep vegetal slate (`#13241C`) to secure WCAG AAA legibility against the cream backdrop.

## Typography

The design system relies on **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all hierarchies. Its open counters, clean geometric proportions, and generous x-height make it exceptionally legible on handheld screens under varying ambient lighting conditions.

- **Headlines:** Use weights `700` and `800` with subtle negative letter-spacing for grounded, confident titles that structure tour listings and experience packages.
- **Body Content:** Generous line heights (`26px` on `16px` base) preserve effortless reading during prolonged mobile browsing.
- **Labels & Tags:** Rendered in `600` and `700` weights for instant glanceability across travel badges, price callouts, and route tags.

## Layout & Spacing

A mobile-first fluid layout forms the backbone of the experience:

- **Mobile Canvas (<640px):** Single-column layout with `1rem` screen margin and `1rem` vertical item gap, maximizing horizontal card surface for high-resolution destination photography.
- **Tablet & Landscape (640px - 1024px):** Adapts to a 6-to-8 column fluid grid using `1.5rem` gutters and `2rem` screen margins.
- **Desktop (>1024px):** Locks content to a centered container (`max-width: 1200px`) with 12 columns, ensuring cards maintain legible horizontal proportions without expansive distortion.
- **Thumb Reach Zone:** Primary actions, floating reservation bars, and navigation chips reside strictly within bottom thumb reach (`bottom-0` anchored or bottom-sheet flows).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through gentle natural light shadows layered over warm organic containers, avoiding artificial dark drops:

- **Base Ground:** Natural `#FAF7F0` (warm cream) canvas.
- **Surface Elevation (Cards & Sheets):** Pure `#FFFFFF` card surfaces hovering over the cream base, accompanied by an extra-diffused, forest-tinted ambient drop shadow:
  - `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(27, 77, 62, 0.07), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(27, 77, 62, 0.04);`
- **Floating Modals & Bottom Navigation:**
  - `box-shadow: 0 10px 30px -4px rgba(19, 36, 28, 0.12);`
- **Interactive Depress State:** Elements scale down slightly (`transform: scale(0.98)`) with zero shadow upon tap to simulate a soft mechanical tactile response.

## Shapes

The interface embraces inviting, organic contours reflecting the rolling hillside geography:

- **Cards & Sheets (`rounded-2xl` / `1rem` to `1.5rem`):** Destination thumbnails, package summaries, and bottom sheets use full `rounded-2xl` curvature for an approachable, modern feel.
- **Buttons (`rounded-xl` to full pill `rounded-full`):** Floating interaction items and chips use pill structures, while full-width mobile action buttons use generous `1rem` radii.
- **Image Frames:** Photographic elements always share the bounding card's `rounded-2xl` radius with seamless `overflow-hidden` edge clipping.

## Components

### Buttons (Touch-Friendly)
- **Primary Action (Pesan Sekarang / Booking):** Minimum height `54px` (exceeding standard 48px touch targets for thumb comfort). Solid `#1B4D3E` fill with `#FFFFFF` label-lg text and soft elevation.
- **Secondary Action (Hubungi Pengelola / WhatsApp):** Outline `#1B4D3E` (1.5px solid) with `#FAF7F0` surface or soft gold tint (`rgba(217, 155, 38, 0.1)`). Minimum height `50px`.
- **Icon Actions:** Minimum touch boundary of `48px × 48px` with centered `24px` iconography.

### Chips & Filter Pills
- Horizontal scrollable row with hidden scrollbars and snapping behavior.
- Inactive state: `#FFFFFF` fill, 1px border `rgba(27, 77, 62, 0.12)`, text `#13241C`.
- Active state: `#1B4D3E` fill, `#FFFFFF` text, subtle green glow shadow.

### Cards (Paket Wisata & Homestay)
- Surface `#FFFFFF` encased in `rounded-2xl` geometry.
- Aspect ratio 16:10 for photographic banners, paired with a floating badge on the top-right corner.
- Bottom padding `1.25rem` to accommodate dual-column price metadata and immediate booking triggers.

### Badges & Tags (Indonesian Traveler Context)
- **Halal Culinary Tag:** Soft green tint (`#E8F5E9`), green text (`#1B4D3E`), icon `verified`.
- **Weather / Highland Warning (Sejuk/Dingin):** Soft blue-teal badge indicating average temperature (e.g., "18°C - 22°C").
- **Family Friendly ("Ramah Anak & Lansia"):** Warm amber tint (`#FFF8E1`), gold text (`#A06800`).
- Border radius is locked to full pill (`9999px`) with micro-label typography (`label-sm`).

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Height `52px` with integrated iconography for date pickers and guest counts.
- Background `#FFFFFF`, border 1.5px `rgba(27, 77, 62, 0.15)`. Focus transitions border to `#1B4D3E` with a 3px outer glow ring of `rgba(46, 125, 90, 0.15)`.

### Bottom Floating Booking Bar
- Pinned bottom bar on mobile screens featuring split-pane structure: price display on the left (e.g., "Mulai Rp 150.000 / malam") and a prominent CTA button on the right, housed on a semi-translucent frosted cream bar (`backdrop-blur-md`).
